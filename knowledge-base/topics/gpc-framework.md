# GPC Reference Context for Data Profiling

This file is a practical reference for interpreting **GPC reference numbers** and **GPC reporting frameworks** when profiling emissions datasets.

Standard: *Global Protocol for Community-Scale Greenhouse Gas Emission Inventories (GPC)*

## Why this file exists

When a dataset contains fields such as `gpc_reference_number`, `scope`, `sector`, or `framework`, it is easy to confuse:

- the **emission source code** itself
- the **scope** of the emission
- the **reporting framework** used for totals
- whether a source is **inside** or **outside** the city boundary
- whether a source is **included in BASIC/BASIC+ totals** or only reported separately

This file separates those ideas so we can profile data more reliably.

## 1. How to read a GPC reference number

Example: `I.2.3`

- `I` = major sector
- `2` = sub-sector within that major sector
- `3` = source/scope variant within that sub-sector

In practice:

- `I` = Stationary Energy
- `II` = Transportation
- `III` = Waste
- `IV` = Industrial Processes and Product Use (IPPU)
- `V` = Agriculture, Forestry and Other Land Use (AFOLU)
- `VI` = Other Scope 3

Example interpretations:

- `I.1.1` = residential buildings, scope 1 fuel combustion inside the city
- `II.1.3` = on-road transportation, scope 3 transboundary share outside the city plus electricity transmission/distribution losses
- `III.1.2` = waste generated in the city but disposed outside the city

## 2. What the framework terms mean

### Scope 1

Emissions that **physically occur within the city boundary**.  
This is also often called **territorial** emissions.

### Scope 2

Emissions from **grid-supplied energy consumed within the city boundary**.  
The emissions may physically occur outside the city, but are attributed to local electricity or grid energy use.

### Scope 3

Selected **out-of-boundary** emissions caused by in-city activity.  
In GPC this is **limited**, not a full consumption-based inventory.

### Territorial total

The territorial total is the sum of **all scope 1 sources**.  
Important: this can include sources that are **not counted in BASIC/BASIC+ totals**.

Examples:

- electricity generation supplied to the grid (`I.4.4`) is scope 1 and belongs in the territorial total
- imported waste treated inside the city (`III.1.3`, `III.2.3`, `III.3.3`, `III.4.3`) is scope 1 and belongs in the territorial total
- those same sources are **excluded from BASIC/BASIC+ totals**

### BASIC

BASIC includes:

- all scope 1 emissions from Stationary Energy, except energy generation supplied to the grid
- all scope 1 emissions from Transportation
- all scope 1 emissions from Waste, except imported waste treated inside the city
- all scope 2 emissions from Stationary Energy
- all scope 2 emissions from Transportation
- scope 3 emissions from **exported waste** treated outside the city

BASIC does **not** include:

- scope 1 emissions from IPPU
- scope 1 emissions from AFOLU
- stationary energy scope 3 transmission/distribution losses
- transportation scope 3 transboundary journeys
- imported waste treated inside the city
- energy generation supplied to the grid

### BASIC+

BASIC+ includes **everything in BASIC**, plus:

- all scope 1 emissions from IPPU
- all scope 1 emissions from AFOLU
- scope 3 transmission/distribution losses from Stationary Energy
- scope 3 Transportation emissions from transboundary journeys and related grid-loss accounting

BASIC+ still does **not** include:

- electricity generation supplied to the grid (`I.4.4`) in the BASIC+ total
- imported waste treated inside the city in the BASIC+ total
- arbitrary consumption-based emissions outside the specific GPC scope 3 sources

### Other Scope 3

`VI` is a bucket for **additional scope 3 emissions** beyond the standard GPC source list.  
These should be disclosed separately and **not mixed into BASIC/BASIC+ totals** unless a methodology explicitly says so.

### Consumption-based accounting

This is **not the same thing** as GPC scope 3 or BASIC+.  
Consumption-based accounting estimates emissions from goods and services consumed by residents or organizations, regardless of where the emissions occurred. The GPC mentions it as a complementary approach, not the same as the standard city inventory totals.

## 3. Profiling rules of thumb

Use these when classifying or cleaning records:

1. A `gpc_reference_number` identifies an **emission source category**, not a full methodology by itself.
2. Scope 1 does **not automatically mean BASIC/BASIC+ included**.
3. Waste is special:
   - BASIC/BASIC+ follows **waste generated by the city**
   - territorial scope 1 follows **waste treated inside the city**
4. IPPU and AFOLU are mostly **scope 1 only** in the core GPC tables.
5. Electricity used by waste, IPPU, or AFOLU activities is often reported under **Stationary Energy**, not under those sectors.
6. Transportation scope 3 mainly covers **the out-of-boundary share of transboundary trips**.
7. Stationary Energy scope 3 mainly covers **transmission and distribution losses**, not broad upstream fuel lifecycle emissions.
8. If a dataset only reports `scope`, it is less precise than a dataset with `gpc_reference_number`.

## 5. Code-by-code reference

The tables below describe what each GPC code generally **includes** and **does not include** for profiling purposes.

## I. Stationary Energy

### I.1 Residential buildings

| Code | Meaning | Includes | Does not include |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `I.1.1` | Scope 1 fuel combustion in residential buildings | On-site burning of fuels in homes inside the city | Grid electricity use, grid-loss emissions, emissions outside city boundary |
| `I.1.2` | Scope 2 grid energy used in residential buildings | Electricity or other grid-supplied energy consumed by homes in the city | On-site fuel combustion, transmission/distribution losses |
| `I.1.3` | Scope 3 grid transmission/distribution losses for residential use | Losses associated with delivering grid energy consumed by homes | Direct household combustion, broad upstream fuel supply-chain emissions |

### I.2 Commercial and institutional buildings and facilities

| Code | Meaning | Includes | Does not include |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `I.2.1` | Scope 1 fuel combustion in commercial/institutional buildings | On-site fuel use in offices, schools, hospitals, public buildings, facilities | Grid electricity use, T&D losses |
| `I.2.2` | Scope 2 grid energy used in commercial/institutional buildings | Electricity or grid energy consumed by those buildings | On-site combustion, T&D losses |
| `I.2.3` | Scope 3 grid transmission/distribution losses for commercial/institutional use | Grid-loss emissions associated with `I.2.2` consumption | Direct combustion, non-grid upstream emissions |

### I.3 Manufacturing industries and construction

| Code | Meaning | Includes | Does not include |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `I.3.1` | Scope 1 fuel combustion in manufacturing and construction | Energy-related combustion in factories and construction activity inside the city | Process emissions from chemical or physical transformation, grid use, T&D losses |
| `I.3.2` | Scope 2 grid energy used in manufacturing and construction | Electricity or grid energy used by industrial and construction sites | On-site combustion, process emissions, T&D losses |
| `I.3.3` | Scope 3 grid transmission/distribution losses for manufacturing and construction | Grid-loss emissions tied to `I.3.2` consumption | Direct combustion, IPPU process emissions |

### I.4 Energy industries

| Code | Meaning | Includes | Does not include |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `I.4.1` | Scope 1 energy used in power plant auxiliary operations | Fuel combustion for support operations at in-city power plants | Electricity generated for export to the grid itself |
| `I.4.2` | Scope 2 grid energy used in power plant auxiliary operations | Grid energy consumed by auxiliary plant operations | On-site auxiliary fuel combustion, generated electricity output |
| `I.4.3` | Scope 3 grid transmission/distribution losses for auxiliary operations | Grid-loss emissions tied to `I.4.2` | Plant combustion, generated electricity supplied to users |
| `I.4.4` | Scope 1 emissions from energy generation supplied to the grid | Emissions from in-city electricity or energy generation exported to the grid | Auxiliary energy already captured in `I.4.1`/`I.4.2`; this is reported in territorial scope 1 but excluded from BASIC/BASIC+ totals |

### I.5 Agriculture, forestry and fishing activities

| Code | Meaning | Includes | Does not include |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `I.5.1` | Scope 1 fuel combustion in agriculture/forestry/fishing activities | Fuel burned by these activities inside the city | AFOLU biological or land-use emissions, grid energy, T&D losses |
| `I.5.2` | Scope 2 grid energy used in agriculture/forestry/fishing activities | Electricity or grid energy consumed by these activities | Fuel combustion, AFOLU non-energy emissions |
| `I.5.3` | Scope 3 grid transmission/distribution losses for agriculture/forestry/fishing energy use | Grid-loss emissions tied to `I.5.2` | AFOLU land or livestock emissions |

### I.6 Non-specified sources

| Code | Meaning | Includes | Does not include |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `I.6.1` | Scope 1 fuel combustion in unspecified stationary energy sources | Stationary fuel use that cannot be assigned to a more specific stationary energy sub-sector | Transportation, waste, IPPU, AFOLU, grid use |
| `I.6.2` | Scope 2 grid energy in unspecified stationary energy sources | Grid energy use that cannot be assigned to a more specific stationary energy sub-sector | Direct combustion, T&D losses |
| `I.6.3` | Scope 3 grid transmission/distribution losses for unspecified stationary energy use | Grid-loss emissions tied to `I.6.2` | Any direct combustion or non-grid upstream emissions |

### I.7 Fugitive emissions from coal systems

| Code | Meaning | Includes | Does not include |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `I.7.1` | Scope 1 fugitive emissions from coal mining, processing, storage, and transport | Methane or other fugitive releases from coal systems inside the city | Fuel combustion emissions, oil and gas fugitives |

### I.8 Fugitive emissions from oil and natural gas systems

| Code | Meaning | Includes | Does not include |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `I.8.1` | Scope 1 fugitive emissions from oil and natural gas systems | Leaks and fugitive releases inside the city from oil/gas extraction, processing, storage, or distribution systems | Fuel combustion emissions, coal fugitives |

### II.1 On-road transportation

| Code | Meaning | Includes | Does not include |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `II.1.1` | Scope 1 on-road fuel combustion inside the city | Tailpipe combustion from road transport occurring within the boundary | Out-of-boundary share of transboundary trips, grid electricity use, grid losses |

| `II.1.3` | Scope 3 out-of-boundary share of transboundary road trips plus grid losses | Portion of road journeys occurring outside the city and T&D losses from electricity used for road transport | In-boundary road emissions already counted in `II.1.1`/`II.1.2` |

### II.2 Railways

| Code | Meaning | Includes | Does not include |

| `II.2.3` | Scope 3 out-of-boundary share of transboundary rail trips plus grid losses | Rail journey share outside the city and electricity T&D losses | In-boundary railway activity already counted elsewhere |

| Code | Meaning | Includes | Does not include |

| `II.3.3` | Scope 3 out-of-boundary share of transboundary water journeys plus grid losses | Journey portions outside the city and T&D losses from electricity use | In-boundary navigation activity already counted elsewhere |

| Code | Meaning | Includes | Does not include |

| `II.4.3` | Scope 3 out-of-boundary share of transboundary aviation plus grid losses | Flight portions outside the city and electricity T&D losses | In-boundary aviation activity already counted in scope 1 or 2 |

| Code | Meaning | Includes | Does not include |

## III. Waste

Important profiling rule: for Waste, **scope 1 follows where treatment happens**, but **BASIC/BASIC+ follows where the waste was generated**.

### III.1 Solid waste disposal

| Code | Meaning | Includes | Does not include |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `III.1.1` | Waste generated in the city and disposed in-city | Landfill or open-dump emissions from locally generated waste treated inside the city | Imported waste, exported waste |
| `III.1.2` | Waste generated in the city and disposed outside the city | Exported municipal waste treated or disposed outside the city | Waste treated inside the city |
| `III.1.3` | Waste generated outside the city and disposed in-city | Imported waste treated inside the city | Waste generated by the city; excluded from BASIC/BASIC+ totals but included in territorial scope 1 |

### III.2 Biological treatment of waste

| Code | Meaning | Includes | Does not include |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `III.2.1` | City-generated waste biologically treated in-city | Composting or biological treatment of local waste inside the city | Imported or exported waste |
| `III.2.2` | City-generated waste biologically treated outside the city | Exported waste biologically treated outside the city | In-city treated waste |
| `III.2.3` | Outside-generated waste biologically treated in-city | Imported waste biologically treated within the city | Local waste; excluded from BASIC/BASIC+ totals but included in territorial scope 1 |

### III.3 Incineration and open burning

| Code | Meaning | Includes | Does not include |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `III.3.1` | Waste generated and treated in-city by incineration/open burning | Local waste incinerated or openly burned within the city | Exported or imported waste treated elsewhere or inside the city respectively |
| `III.3.2` | City-generated waste incinerated/open-burned outside the city | Exported waste treated outside the city by incineration or open burning | In-city treatment |
| `III.3.3` | Outside-generated waste incinerated/open-burned in-city | Imported waste treated inside the city | Local waste; excluded from BASIC/BASIC+ totals but included in territorial scope 1 |

### III.4 Wastewater treatment and discharge

| Code | Meaning | Includes | Does not include |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `III.4.1` | Wastewater generated and treated in-city | Wastewater from inside the city treated within the city | Imported or exported wastewater |
| `III.4.2` | Wastewater generated in-city but treated outside the city | Exported wastewater treated outside the city | In-city treatment |
| `III.4.3` | Wastewater generated outside the city but treated in-city | Imported wastewater treated within the city | Local wastewater; excluded from BASIC/BASIC+ totals but included in territorial scope 1 |

## IV. Industrial Processes and Product Use (IPPU)

Important profiling rule: IPPU covers **process and product-use emissions**, not energy combustion.  
If the emission comes from burning fuel for heat or power, it usually belongs in **Stationary Energy**, not IPPU.

| Code | Meaning | Includes | Does not include |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `IV.1` | Industrial processes within the city boundary | Process emissions from chemical or physical transformation of materials in-city, such as cement, lime, chemicals, metals, etc. | Fuel combustion for energy, grid electricity use, out-of-boundary industrial emissions |
| `IV.2` | Product use within the city boundary | Emissions from use of products such as refrigerants, aerosols, solvents, fluorinated-gas applications, and similar product-use categories inside the city | Fuel combustion for energy, generic consumption-based product footprints outside the core GPC definition |

## V. Agriculture, Forestry and Other Land Use (AFOLU)

Important profiling rule: AFOLU covers **biological and land-use emissions**, not the electricity or fuel used by farm buildings or vehicles. Those energy emissions are usually reported under **Stationary Energy** or **Transportation**.

| Code | Meaning | Includes | Does not include |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `V.1` | Livestock within the city boundary | Methane and other GHG emissions from livestock activities inside the city | Fuel use in agricultural equipment, electricity use in farm buildings |
| `V.2` | Land within the city boundary | Emissions and removals associated with land use and land-use change within the city | Agricultural energy consumption, imported agricultural products |
| `V.3` | Aggregate and non-CO2 land sources within the city boundary | Additional AFOLU land-related non-CO2 and aggregate land emission sources within the city | Energy combustion, out-of-boundary land-use effects |

## VI. Other Scope 3

| Code | Meaning | Includes | Does not include |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `VI` | Other Scope 3 sources outside the standard GPC source list | Optional additional out-of-boundary sources disclosed separately, potentially including broader supply-chain or consumption-linked estimates if methodologically documented | Anything that should already be mapped to sectors `I` through `V`; these sources should not be silently rolled into BASIC/BASIC+ totals |

## 7. Recommended profiling fields

If we are profiling a dataset, these derived flags are useful:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `gpc_sector` | Major sector: `I`, `II`, `III`, `IV`, `V`, `VI` |
| `gpc_subsector` | Sub-sector code such as `I.2` or `III.4` |
| `gpc_scope` | `1`, `2`, `3`, or `null` for non-leaf umbrella labels |
| `is_basic_included` | Whether the code contributes to BASIC totals |
| `is_basic_plus_included` | Whether the code contributes to BASIC+ totals |
| `is_territorial_scope1` | Whether the code is part of the territorial total |
| `is_imported_waste` | True for `III.1.3`, `III.2.3`, `III.3.3`, `III.4.3` |
| `is_exported_waste` | True for `III.1.2`, `III.2.2`, `III.3.2`, `III.4.2` |
| `is_transboundary_transport` | True for `II.1.3`, `II.2.3`, `II.3.3`, `II.4.3` |
| `is_grid_loss_scope3` | True for `I.*.3`, `II.*.3`, and possibly `I.4.3` depending on treatment |

## 8. Common mistakes to avoid

- Do not assume all scope 1 sources are counted in BASIC/BASIC+ totals.
- Do not treat `III.1.3` style imported-waste records as city-generated waste.
- Do not map IPPU process emissions into Stationary Energy just because an industrial facility is involved.
- Do not map industrial fuel combustion into IPPU if the emission is energy-related rather than process-related.
- Do not treat all scope 3 as consumption-based accounting.
- Do not assume `VI` is standardized to the same level of granularity as sectors `I` to `V`.

## 9. Best-use summary

For data profiling, the safest interpretation is:

- `gpc_reference_number` tells you the **specific emissions source**
- `scope` tells you **where the emissions occur relative to the city**
- `BASIC` and `BASIC+` tell you **which sources belong in published totals**
- `territorial` tells you **all in-boundary scope 1 emissions**
- `Other Scope 3` and consumption-based estimates should usually be treated as **supplementary**, not core-inventory totals
