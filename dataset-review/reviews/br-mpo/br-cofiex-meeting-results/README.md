# COFIEX/MPO — external-financing meeting results

This review covers the project tables from the 180th, 182nd, 184th, 185th and 187th COFIEX meetings held from June 2025 through June 2026. The working release contains 138 financing decisions: 80 approvals and 58 non-approvals. **Status: source extracts, cleaned release, validation, reviewed English translations and a draft adaptation mapping are complete; legal review and mapping adjudication remain pending. Nothing is production-approved.**

## Canonical downloads

The canonical sources are the official per-meeting result PDFs published by the Ministério do Planejamento e Orçamento **(verified)**. They preserve approvals, rejections, requested or approved amounts, proponent, lender and decision reasons. Three meetings reconcile to published counts or totals; the 185th only partly reconciles and the 187th has no independent control **(verified from the files)**.

The preferred alternative for approved operations is the matching Diário Oficial COFIEX resolution. Official acts are outside copyright protection under Lei 9.610 article 8 IV **(inferred application; legal confirmation pending)**. Resolutions cannot replace the meeting results for the 58 rejected operations, so the result PDFs remain necessary if rejection evidence is in scope.

## License

The ministry pages carry the gov.br CC BY-ND 3.0 footer and the PDFs contain no separate data licence **(verified)**. Whether normalising and translating the tables is permitted under the federal free-use rule or official-act exclusion is unresolved. Row-level redistribution remains under review.

## Spatial and temporal scope

The decisions cover federal entities, public companies, states and municipalities across Brazil. Amounts remain in BRL, USD or EUR as published; some source tables themselves convert other currencies. A meeting approval authorises preparation of an external-financing operation and possible Union guarantee processing. It is not lender approval, contract signature or disbursement.

## Interpretation warnings

Approved and rejected decisions belong in the same analytical universe but must not be summed as financed money. Partial approvals use the approved amount where the bulletin provides it. Repeated proponents and projects across meetings can represent resubmissions rather than separate projects.

## Parsing notes

The release uses English field names and preserves all 114 distinct Portuguese project descriptions in `project_description_pt`. The paired English descriptions use an exact release dictionary in the restartable build, and all 138 rows carry `translation_status=complete_human_reviewed_translation`. Portuguese notes remain source text in `notes_pt` and were not translated. No reliable native operation identifier is printed across all meeting tables, so the release key is synthetic and cross-meeting entity resolution remains analytical.
