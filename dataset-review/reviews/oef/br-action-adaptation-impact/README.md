# Brazil action-to-adaptation-risk mapping

This research product connects adaptation actions to the vulnerability and exposure
indicators of six AdaptaBrasil sectors. Direct indicator relationships proceed to an
evidence search and a component-level effectiveness claim.

- Method: [`methodology.md`](methodology.md)
- V1 structure and rerun instructions: [`releases/v1/README.md`](releases/v1/README.md)
- Proposed V1 dataset: [`releases/v1/output/action_risk_assessments.json`](releases/v1/output/action_risk_assessments.json)

V1 is awaiting human review and is not production approved.
