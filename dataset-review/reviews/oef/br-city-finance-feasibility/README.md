# OEF — Brazil city finance feasibility (br-city-finance-feasibility)

The Brazil counterpart of the Chile city-action fundability model: for a Brazilian municipality and a climate action, which finance pathways are evidenced, what stage of finance the evidence represents, and what city-side conditions still need checking. This entry is an internal derived product: every source review remains authoritative for provenance, interpretation and licence, and this entry re-grants nothing.

Status: release v1 preserves the original 53-action pilot model and its 50-city outputs. Release v2 replaces its action input with the supplied V13 final action bank. Cost band is the only financing field used to order actions. All other finance information is context for the city and the project-preparation module. The catalogue draws on ten source datasets and six finance routes. The committed tables hold 2,623 records and 2,700 illustrative project-to-action matches from sources cleared for redistribution; Novo PAC, COFIEX and CGU records (3,700 and 3,780 in total) are kept in a gitignored table for internal analysis until their reuse terms are resolved. Match type, strength and review status remain explicit; no match is a final implementation recommendation.

## Why we use it

- Feeds the Feasibility pillar (Financing component) of the climate-action prioritisation methodology for Brazil.
- Separates evidence that a pathway operates from eligibility for a particular city or action.
- Preserves v1 as the historical pilot while v2 migrates to the replacement action bank and reviewed finance sources.

No release is production-approved. Release **v1** is the preserved pilot. Release **v2** is the current research release and contains the finance-context catalogue; mapping adjudication and city-level eligibility remain pending.
