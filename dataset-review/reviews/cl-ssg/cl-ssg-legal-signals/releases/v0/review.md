# Quick Methodology Summary

- This review scores each waste-sector action on municipal viability in Chile using legal, governance, and financing evidence.
- Total score is weighted: `Legal 40% + Governance 30% + Financing 30%`.
- Subcriteria are scored on a discrete scale: `100` (fully meets), `50` (partially meets), `0` (does not meet).
- Interpretation bands: `>=75` viable, `40-74` viable with conditions, `<40` low current viability.

# Key Insights from This Review

- **Legal assessment workflow:** The most reliable approach is action-first (start from each action, then assess legal basis, interactions across laws, scoring, and references). Going law-first (extracting all legal documents first and then inferring action assessments) was not recommended as a reliable method.
- **Governance scoring scope:** Governance is currently benchmarked against a typical municipality, but city-level variation is significant (for example rural/urban context, density, and local capacity differences).
- **Governance next step (v2):** SSG will share municipality categorisation criteria to support a more accurate profile-based governance scoring approach in a future v2 review.
- **Current governance limitation:** The present governance benchmark is broad for model use and may create false signaling if treated as city-specific evidence.
- **Finance scoring direction:** The financing dimension may be replaced or complemented by a rules-based matching approach using the funding database, reducing dependence on expert-only review.
