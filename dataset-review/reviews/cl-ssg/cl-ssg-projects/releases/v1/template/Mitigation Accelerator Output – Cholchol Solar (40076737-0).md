## **3. Preferred Mitigation Action – Technical Summary**

* **Name of selected action:** Expand solar energy generation on municipal facilities and public assets (`icare_0012`).
* **Action description** (library, EN, verbatim): *"The city will install and scale up solar
  photovoltaic (PV) systems on municipal buildings and public assets, including schools, health
  centers, administrative offices, bus terminals, water treatment plants, and decommissioned
  landfills. The objective is to increase the share of renewable electricity in municipal operations,
  lower energy costs, and reduce greenhouse gas emissions…"*
* **Project components** (FICHA, dataset translation): *"a) Strengthen technical and energy
  management capacities in the population; b) implement renewable and efficient PV solutions in
  productive units with infrastructure and specialized assistance."*
* **Project description** (FICHA, dataset translation): *"The initiative seeks to implement clean and
  sustainable energy solutions in 71 rural and urban productive units in Cholchol, combining the
  installation of photovoltaic solar systems with technical training and specialized assistance. Its
  purpose is to reduce operational costs, increase competitiveness, and close territorial and social
  gaps in access to energy … The strategy is articulated in two complementary components:
  (1) strengthening capacities and energy management — in-person workshops, practical modules, and
  personalized support in operation, maintenance, and efficient use of renewable technologies; and
  (2) renewable and efficient infrastructure — installation of photovoltaic systems in units
  prioritized for energy vulnerability and productive potential, executed by certified contractors
  under municipal supervision, with ongoing maintenance support. The municipality of Cholchol will
  lead execution in coordination with the regional government … Resources will be executed through
  public bidding."*
* **Capacity / scale:** 71 productive units (FICHA); project duration **12 months**.
* **GPC reference:** subsector I.4 (`I.4.4`). Sector / subsector (source): Energy / Self-generation.
