# AdaptaBrasil (MCTI) — climate risk indicators

National platform from Brazil’s Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation (MCTI) that integrates climate change impact risk indices and indicators across strategic sectors (water, food security, energy, health, transport, biodiversity, geo-hydrological disasters, and others).

## Why we track it

- **Subnational climate risk** — indicators at municipality and other administrative resolutions support CCRA-style and adaptation planning use cases.
- **Programmatic access** — JSON APIs and bulk geometry/tabular downloads documented by the publisher and mirrored in the community access repo.
- **Pipeline candidate** — API-based ingestion is feasible; hierarchy and indicator metadata should be refreshed when the platform updates.

## Current approved release

**v1** (dataset review folder; production approval is tracked in `catalog/index.yaml`).
