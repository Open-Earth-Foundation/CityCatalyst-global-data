## What the pilot demonstrates

- Intervention wording matters. Coastal policy is not automatically coastal-infrastructure policy.
- Partial functional matches remain visible. Temporary accommodation supports shelter outcomes without satisfying the full action definition.
- A no-match result is valid and should be retained.
- Strong alignment requires direct evidence plus supporting policy detail.
- Scores summarize reviewed evidence; they do not establish delivery or effectiveness.

## Review cautions

- The urban-drainage score is close to the strong threshold. Removing the broader municipal-support record would lower it to medium, so this boundary should be reviewed before scaling.
- Machine-translated English supports review but is not official wording. Portuguese evidence and page references remain attached.
- The Strategy and executive-summary copies of national targets are not counted twice.
