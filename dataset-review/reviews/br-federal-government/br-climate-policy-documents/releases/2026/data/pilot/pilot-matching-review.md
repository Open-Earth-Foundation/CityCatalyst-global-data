# Seven-action pilot review

## Results

| Action | Score | Grade | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Strengthen climate resilience in farming and fisheries | 83.1 | Strong | Quantified national targets, resilience credit, and aquaculture practices directly support the action. |
| Support livelihood diversification for climate resilience | 44.4 | Medium | Productive inclusion, income generation, and autonomy are supported, but fisheries-specific alternative livelihoods are not explicit. |
| Strengthen the healthcare system for extreme events | 80.3 | Strong | Resilient infrastructure, teams, procedures, hospital planning, and continuity of care are directly addressed. |
| Public shading in heat hotspots | 0.0 | None | No meaningful evidence in the selected corpus. |
| Increase water capture and storage | 84.3 | Strong | A quantified technology target and direct implementation commitments cover productive and household systems. |
| Improve efficient water use/demand | 90.5 | Strong | Localized irrigation, reuse, efficient aquaculture, and reduced industrial abstraction are directly supported. |
| Improve watershed protection and management | 84.7 | Strong | Basin revitalization, source protection, restoration, governance, and implementation projects directly align. |

## What the pilot demonstrates

- Intervention wording matters. General income generation is not automatically fisheries-specific livelihood diversification.
- Partial functional matches remain visible. Productive inclusion and economic autonomy support livelihood resilience without satisfying the full action definition.
- A no-match result is valid and should be retained.
- Strong alignment is supported here by direct targets and implementation commitments across sector plans.
- Scores summarize reviewed evidence; they do not establish delivery or effectiveness.

## Review cautions

- The livelihood-diversification score is intentionally capped at medium because none of the accepted records explicitly shifts fisheries- or mariculture-dependent households into alternative sectors.
- Machine-translated English supports review but is not official wording. Portuguese evidence and page references remain attached.
- Duplicate summary copies of national targets are not counted twice.

## Decision needed

Confirm whether the four-grade interpretation and the partial-match treatment for livelihood diversification are reasonable before scaling the method.
